# Momentum Companion · five-task soldier usability check

**Purpose:** Find where soldiers succeed, get stuck or see practical value, before designing new features. Use the live review link only after its deployment has been verified.

## For the participant

Use your own phone or a test browser. Do not enter real medical details, classified service information, identity numbers, contact information or financial account numbers. Invented example data is fine. The observer should not request passwords.

Try these tasks in order, saying aloud what you expect to happen:

1. From **Home**, find an updated benefit for a discharged soldier. Can you identify the responsible official source, the verification date and what you need to check about eligibility?
2. From **Home**, prepare a resume. Find the editable CV draft, create or edit one, and locate the existing download option. Check whether the draft asks you to verify statements.
3. From a chat, press **Home**, then return to your chat. Next locate **Previous conversations** and select an earlier conversation. Does anything you typed or saved disappear?
4. From **Home**, compare two imaginary education/training options. Enter a duration, cost and official-source URL for each; see them together in the comparison.
5. From **Home**, save a follow-up action (for example, "ask the institution about admission requirements"). Find the saved step and mark it completed.

The optional Home testing section can record completed tasks, difficulties and value, and download a plain-text feedback report. It is **never automatically sent to Momentum**. Do not record names or any personal data in that report.

After the five tasks, ask: **"What would make you open this again next week instead of ChatGPT?"** and **"What was most difficult to find?"** Observe before explaining; do not lead the participant to a button. Ask consent before taking notes. Do not record screen, voice, or personal content without a separate explicit agreement.

## Decision rules

- Fix a broken or misleading task before adding an adjacent feature.
- Prioritize repeated blockers over visual polish. Distinguish "could not discover" from "feature does not exist".
- Review results across Hebrew and English, small Android and iPhone screens, Wi-Fi and cellular, and new vs returning users.
- Protect the current browser-stored profile and chat in every release. This worksheet is not proof of clinical, legal, security or production readiness.
